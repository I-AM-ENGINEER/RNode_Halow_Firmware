ACK (ARQ) Settings

Per-DATA-packet ACK, stop-and-wait per node. DATA frames carry no header (legacy-compatible); a 3-byte ACK [magic][evm] is sent on every received frame at MCS10.

0 = NOACK (fire-and-forget). Per-peer budget slides −1 when a frame gets no ACK at all, snaps back to max on any ACK.

Wait for ACK before each retransmit. Stop-and-wait ARQ rate ≈ 1000/timeout frames/s per peer.

Privacy

MAC rotation periodically generates a new random source address, making your device harder to track across sessions. The anonymous source MAC sets all transmitted frames to FF:FF:FF:FF:FF:FF. These two options are mutually exclusive — enabling one will disable the other.

Important: MAC rotation is not recommended for stationary nodes. Changing your address may break delivery confirmations for established links. The anonymous MAC (FF:FF:FF:FF:FF:FF) disables delivery confirmations entirely, which may reduce link reliability. MAC rotation is primarily useful for portable nodes. Neither random MAC nor rotation makes you invisible on the radio channel — with an SDR and a directional antenna, there is no difference in time to detect the signal source.
